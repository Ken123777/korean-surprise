KOREAN SURPRISE

Korean food finds · what · where · how much

Korean Surprise is a YouTube channel of short videos about Korean food for people visiting Korea: street food, market stalls and local favorites, each with what the dish is, where to find it and how much it costs.

Watch the channel on YouTube: youtube.com/@koreansurprise

Korean Surprise Uploader

Korean Surprise Uploader is the channel's own publishing tool. It edits the channel's short videos and uploads them to the Korean Surprise YouTube channel using the YouTube API Services, and it reads that channel's view statistics to report back to the channel owner.

The tool is used only by the owner of the Korean Surprise channel, for that channel. It does not offer sign-in to other people and does not access anyone else's account.

Read how it handles data in the Privacy Policy and the rules for using this site in the Terms of Service.

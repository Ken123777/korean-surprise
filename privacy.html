Privacy Policy

Korean Surprise and Korean Surprise Uploader · Last updated October 14, 2026

This policy explains what data Korean Surprise Uploader (the "App") accesses and how it is used. The App is the publishing tool of the Korean Surprise YouTube channel and is used only by the owner of that channel.

YouTube API Services

The App uses the YouTube API Services. By authorizing the App you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the App accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the App accesses only the owner's own Korean Surprise YouTube channel:

  • Basic channel information (channel name, ID, subscriber and video counts), to confirm it is connected to the right channel.
  • Permission to upload videos, titles, descriptions and tags to that channel.
  • View statistics for that channel's videos (YouTube Analytics), to produce performance reports for the owner.

The App does not access any other person's account or data, and it does not collect personal information from viewers.

How data is used, stored and shared

  • The data above is used only to upload the channel's videos and to report their performance to the channel owner.
  • The OAuth access token is stored on infrastructure controlled by the channel owner and is never shared.
  • Data is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising.
  • Analytics data is kept only as long as needed for the owner's reports.

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security settings: myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is revoked, the stored token is deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
